I am currently reading this book for its perspective on how beliefs, habits, and internal narratives shape financial behavior.


What interests me most is not only its discussion of wealth itself, but also its broader ideas about self-limiting patterns, long-term thinking, and personal growth. I see it as a book about mindset as much as money.


Money is extremely important in the field where it functions, extremely unimportant in where it don't(I think that's basically the meaning)


## WealthFile 5 Rich people focus on opportunities

Your field of focus determines what you find in life.
Focus on opportunities and that's what you find.


If you want to get rich, focus on making, keeping, and investing you money. If you want to be poor, focus on spending your money.


If you really want to learn a business, get into it. Get in the corridor by getting a job in the arena.
In this way can you make the contacts you need.


_Action always beats inaction_
Rich people get started. They trust that once they get in the game, they can meke intelligent decisions in the present moment, make corrections, and adjust their sails along the way.
Poor people never take action and therefore always lose.


**I focues on oppurtunities over obstacles!**


## WealthFile #6: Rich people admire other rich and successful people

Thoughts and opinions aren't good or bad, right or wrong, as they enter your mind, but they can sure be empowering and disempowering to your happiness and success, as they enter you life.


When negative energy run through me, I _neutralized_ the negativity in the mind.
**You don't have to be perfect to get rich, but you do need to recognize when your thinking isn't empowering to yourself or others, then quickly refocus on more supportive thoughts**


Try these characteristics on for size: positive, reliable, focused, determined, persistent, hardworking, energetic, good with people, a competent communicator, semi-intelligent, and an expert in at least on area.


when you see people better or good, **bless that which you want**


## 7 Rich people associate with positive, successfule people

Whenever you're introduced to an extremely rich person, you shoud: **create a way to get together with them. you should talk to them, learn how they think, exchange contacts, and if we have other things in common, possibly becom personal friends with them**

Don't bother trying to get negative people to change. Your job is to use what you've learned to better yourself and your life.
You should never live with a person who was negative and pooh-poohed you desire to learn and grow, be it personally, spiritually, or financially. You shouldn't do that to yourself because you respect yourself and your life and you deserve to be as happy and successful as possible.


_people earn within 20% of the average income of their closest friends_
_If you want to fly with the eagles, don't swim with the ducks!_


you should only associate with successful, positive people, and just as importantly, you **disassociate** from negative ones.


Rich people hang around with winners


It's not about "touching" millionaires, it's about deciding that you are ust as good and worthy as they are, and then acting like it.


**stop watching trash TV and stay away from bad news**


## 8 Rich people are willing to promote thmselves and their value. Poor people think negatively about selling and promotion

Resenting promotion is one of the greatest obstacles to success. People who have issues with selling and promotion are usually broke.

Rich people are almost always excellent promoters.
They're skilled at packaging their value in a way that's extremely attractive.

Every business depends on sellings.

Rich people are usuallly leaders, and all great leader are great promoters. To be a leader, you must inherently have followers and supporters.

**Leaders earn a heck of a lot more money than followers!**

## 9 Rich people are bigger than their problems

**The secret to success is not to try to avoid or get rid of or shrink from your problems; the secret is to grow yourself so taht you are bigger than any problem.**

This size of the promblem is nefver the issue-what matters is the size of you!


**If you have a big problem in your life, all taht means is that you are being a small person!** Don't be fooled by appearances, YOUr outer world is merely a reflection of your inner world. If you want to make a permanent chage, stop focusing on the size of your problems and start focusing on the size of you!

The bigger the problems you can handle, the bigger the business you can handle.

Think yourself as your container for wealth. You must grow to be a big container so you cannot only hold more wealth but also attract more wealth.

For rich people, they don't focus on the problem; they focus on their goal. The mind can focus on on predominant thing at a time, so you either whining about the problem or you are working on the solution. Rich and successful people are solution-oriented; they spend thei time and energy strategizing and planning the answers to challenges that come up, and creating sustems to make certain that problem doesn't occur again.

Say: "I am bigger than any problems." " I can handle any problems."

**Whenever you are feeling upset over a "big" problem, point to yourself and say, "Mini me, mini me." Then take a deep breath and say to yourself, "I can handle this. I am bigger than any problem."**
