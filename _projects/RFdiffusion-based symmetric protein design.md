## Overview

This project explored de novo symmetric protein design, focusing on closed repeat proteins generated with modern structure-generation and inverse-folding tools. The work was carried out at SUSTech under the supervision of Prof. Zhe Li.

## What I worked on

I worked on the computational design workflow for generating and evaluating symmetric protein candidates. The project helped me gain practical experience with backbone generation, sequence design, and structure-confidence filtering for de novo protein design.

## Methods / Pipeline

The design workflow combined **RFdiffusion** for backbone generation, **ProteinMPNN** for sequence design, and **SuperFold** for rapid structure prediction and candidate screening. Candidate designs were reviewed using predicted structural confidence and geometric plausibility.

## Research significance

This project strengthened my interest in AI-driven biomolecular design by connecting generative modeling with practical candidate filtering. It also gave me a foundation for later work on linker design and protein-RNA binder design.
