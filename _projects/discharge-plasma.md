This engineering project focused on improving an atomization device for producing air-discharge plasma-activated mist. I designed a magnetic component intended to magnetize plasma-activated water and improve the atomization effect.

The project sits a little outside my current protein-design focus, but it reflects my broader interest in building physical systems and connecting engineering design with experimental validation.
